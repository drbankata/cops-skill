About COPS

People first. Then everything else.

COPS Skilled Services Private Limited is a registered facility management company operating since 2008. We place trained, screened and supervised manpower across the full range of soft services — housekeeping, facade and glass cleaning, horticulture, waste management, pantry and allied support — for corporate offices, hospitals, hotels, retail and residential complexes across Delhi NCR.

What we believe

The quality of the people is the service.

Our strength has always been the quality of the people we put on site. We recruit for skill and bearing, badge every person before deployment, and hold the standard through our own trained field supervisors rather than leaving it to the client to police. Outsourcing soft services to COPS should mean fewer attrition problems, less management time spent on housekeeping, and more of your attention available for your own core work.

COPS is a housekeeping, security, event and facility management services company. We recruit technically sound security and facility management officers, executives, ushers and marshals for sites, events and specialised programmes, and we do not deploy anyone who has not been approved and badged by COPS management.

We are backed by a team of professionals experienced in housekeeping industry recruitment and manpower selection, and we hold ourselves to a quick, effective and timely response on anything you raise.

How we hold the standard

Four things we never compromise on.

  1. Screened & badged

    Every person is approved and badged by COPS management before deployment.

  2. Trained in-house

    Our field supervisors are trained within COPS and carry the standard onto your site.

  3. Recognised

    Recognition and certification programmes reward performers — which keeps attrition low.

  4. Reported

    Consumables, attendance and consumption are reconciled and reported to you every month.

Give-back

Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao.

COPS is a group affiliate of The Indian Chambers of Human Resource. A part of our earnings is directed to the Government of India's Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao mission-mode programmes — our give-back to the nation for women and underprivileged girls.
